# Product Roadmap

## This Week (Week of Sept 2)

### Landing Page ✓ TODO
- Clear headline: what the product does for homecare owners
- One visual showing the problem (missed lead → lost consult)
- One visual showing the solution (quick reply → booked consult)
- CTA: "Join waitlist"
- Zero jargon. Use homecare owner language.

### Waitlist Form ✓ TODO
- Email capture
- Optional: "How many leads do you miss per week?"
- Optional: "Which platform do most leads come from?" (phone, email, web form, etc.)
- Thank you message + set expectations

### Demo Flow ✓ TODO
- **Scenario**: Homecare owner receives an inbound lead at 5pm, team is busy
- **Step 1**: Lead comes in (phone call, email, form submission)
- **Step 2**: System detects: "This lead wasn't answered"
- **Step 3**: System suggests a reply (specific to the lead + homecare's tone)
- **Step 4**: Owner sends reply in 30 seconds
- **Step 5**: Show the follow-up sequence
- Works on desktop AND mobile

### Outreach ✓ TODO
- Record Loom walkthrough of landing page + demo
- Send to 10 homecare owners (with a warm intro)
- Collect: interest level, pain points, willingness to beta

---

## Out of Scope (Do Not Build)

- Payments / Billing
- Full CRM integration (just demo with sample data)
- Admin dashboard
- Multi-user permissions
- Email templates library
- SMS vs. phone vs. email decision logic
- Integrations (Twilio, Zapier, etc.)
