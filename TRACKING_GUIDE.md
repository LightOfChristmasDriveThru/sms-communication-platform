# Outreach Tracking Guide

## How to Use OUTREACH_TRACKER.csv

### Setup (5 minutes)

1. **Open the CSV file** in Excel, Google Sheets, or Numbers
2. **Add your 10 companies** in rows 1-10
3. **Save to Google Drive** (so you can access from phone during calls)
4. **Share the link** with yourself so it's always available

## How to Update During Outreach

### After Sending Email (Same Day)
- [ ] Fill in: Date Sent, Batch
- [ ] Send the email
- [ ] Note any tracking issues (Gmail didn't load, bounced, etc.)

### After First Week (Check opens/responses)
- [ ] Gmail/Mailchimp will show who opened
- [ ] Fill in: Email Opened, Responded, Response Date
- [ ] For anyone who responded: Fill Sentiment, Key Objection
- [ ] Reply to their objection (use OUTREACH_EMAIL_TEMPLATE.md)

### Before/After Call
- [ ] Fill in: Call Scheduled, Call Date
- [ ] **During call:** Take notes (don't fill yet)
- [ ] **After call:** Fill in Call Notes, Willing to Beta, Next Step

### Follow-up Phase
- [ ] Fill in: Follow-up Date
- [ ] Check if they'd be good beta testers
- [ ] Fill in: Final Status

## Status Codes (For Final Status column)

Use one of these:

| Status | Meaning | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Beta Tester | Committed to trying it | Set up their account, provide support |
| Interested | Wants to know more | Schedule a deeper call, send pricing info |
| Warm Lead | Liked the concept | Follow up in 2 weeks, stay in touch |
| Cold | Not interested now | Note why, check in in 6 months |
| Not Qualified | Wrong fit | Note reason, don't follow up |
| Unresponsive | Never replied after 2 attempts | Close it out |

## Daily Workflow

### Monday-Wednesday (Sending Phase)
1. **Morning:** Send Batch 1 emails
2. **Afternoon:** Call any warm intros (same day)
3. **Evening:** Update "Date Sent" column

### Thursday-Friday (Follow-up Phase)
1. **Morning:** Check email opens in Gmail
2. **Midday:** Send 2nd emails to non-responders
3. **Afternoon:** Schedule calls with people who replied
4. **Evening:** Update tracker with responses

### Weekly Review (Every Friday)
1. **Count:** How many watched the video?
2. **Analyze:** What's the #1 objection?
3. **Iterate:** Adjust your pitch for next batch
4. **Schedule:** When are calls happening?

## Key Metrics to Track

As you fill out the tracker, calculate these:

### Response Rate
```
= (# of people who opened email) / 10
```
**Target:** 60%+ for warm intros, 25%+ for cold

### Video Watch Rate
```
= (# of people who watched) / (# who opened email)
```
**Target:** 80%+

### Reply Rate
```
= (# of people who replied) / (# who watched video)
```
**Target:** 40%+

### Call Conversion Rate
```
= (# of calls scheduled) / (# of replies)
```
**Target:** 75%+

### Beta Signup Rate
```
= (# willing to beta) / 10
```
**Target:** 1-3 people

## Tips for Staying Organized

1. **Color-code by status:**
   - 🟢 Green = Warm intros / Beta testers
   - 🟡 Yellow = Waiting for response / Interested
   - 🔴 Red = Cold / Not interested
   - ⚪ Gray = Unresponsive

2. **Sort by Sentiment** (High to Low)
   - Excited → Interested → Not sure → Not relevant

3. **Use conditional formatting**
   - Auto-highlight "Not Responded" if > 5 days old

4. **Print a copy** on Friday for your desk
   - Easier to reference during calls

5. **Back up daily**
   - Save a copy to your computer

---

## What NOT to Do

❌ **Don't** leave a row blank (puts it at the end when sorted)  
❌ **Don't** use "TBD" — either you know or you don't  
❌ **Don't** overwrite old data — add a new entry instead  
❌ **Don't** wait until Friday to update — do it same day  
❌ **Don't** forget to note why someone said "no" — you'll want to revisit later

## Export for Later Analysis

At the end of your outreach (in 3 weeks):

1. **Export to CSV** from Google Sheets
2. **Create a summary:**
   - How many total reached out to?
   - Response rate by source?
   - Most common objection?
   - How many beta testers?

This data will guide your next round of outreach.
