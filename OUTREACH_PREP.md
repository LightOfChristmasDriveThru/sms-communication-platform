# Outreach Preparation: 10 Homecare Owners

## Step 1: Identify Your 10 Targets (2 hours)

### Where to find them:

**Option A: Warm Intros (Best)**
- Your network: Anyone you know in homecare?
- LinkedIn: Search "homecare owner" + your city
- Industry events: Local homecare association meetings
- Referrals: Ask current contacts for 2-3 introductions

**Option B: Cold Outreach (Good)**
- Google: "homecare agency [your city]" + website
- ZoomInfo or Apollo: Filter by homecare, 1-50 employees (owner-operated)
- Facebook Groups: Join homecare owner groups, introduce yourself
- Indeed: Sort homecare agencies by hire volume (growth signals = pain)

### Selection criteria:
- ✅ 1-20 person team (owner still involved in operations)
- ✅ In business 2+ years (not startup, not struggling)
- ✅ Shows signs of growth (hiring, expanding service area)
- ✅ Publicly visible (website, LinkedIn, phone listed)

### RED FLAGS to avoid:
- ❌ Franchise model (corporate decision-makers, not local owner)
- ❌ Purely medical (Medicare/insurance driven, different pain)
- ❌ Recently launched (too early, different priorities)
- ❌ No online presence (won't likely respond to digital outreach)

## Step 5: Send in Batches (Spread over 2 weeks)

### Batch 1: Warm intros (Day 1)
- Send to 3-5 people you know or were referred to
- Personalize each email (mention mutual contact)
- Call them 2 hours after emailing (if you have their number)
- Expected response rate: 60-70%

### Batch 2: Strong prospects (Day 3-4)
- Send to 3-4 companies with clear growth signals
- Mention something specific about them in the email (their hiring post, team size)
- Expected response rate: 20-30%

### Batch 3: Remaining cold prospects (Day 7-8)
- Send to any remaining targets
- Keep it short, one-sentence hook
- Follow up in 3 days
- Expected response rate: 10-15%

### Batch 4: Follow-ups (Days 5, 10, 15)
- Anyone who didn't respond to batch 1/2 gets a follow-up
- Keep the tone warm, not pushy
- "Just checking in, did you get a chance to watch?"

---

## Step 6: Collect & Organize Feedback (Ongoing)

When they respond, save their feedback:

**Use this template:**

```
[Name]

WATCHED: Yes/No
SENTIMENT: Excited / Interested / Not sure / Not relevant
KEY QUOTE: "[What resonated]"

OBJECTION: [If they have concerns]
RESPONSE SENT: [How you addressed it]

NEXT STEP: Wait for feedback / Follow-up call / Beta signup / Not interested
```

**Running tally:**
- How many watched the video?
- What's the #1 objection?
- What's the #1 feature people want?
- Are they willing to beta test?

---

## Step 7: Schedule Follow-up Calls (Optional but Recommended)

For anyone who watches the video and says "this looks interesting":

**Call script (5 minutes):**

> "Hi [Name], thanks for watching the demo. I want to make sure it actually solves your problem. Can you tell me: when a lead comes in after hours, what's your go-to move right now?"
>
> [Listen — this is the key insight]
>
> "Got it. So you'd love to respond faster but you're worried about sounding robotic. That's exactly what we're solving. Here's how it works for a team like yours..."
>
> [Spend 2 min on their specific use case]
>
> "Does that solve it for you?"
>
> [If yes:] "Would you try it for 2 weeks if I set it up for your team?"

## What Success Looks Like

✅ **Week 1:** 5+ people watched the demo  
✅ **Week 2:** 2-3 people want to try it / schedule a call  
✅ **Week 3:** 1 person willing to beta test  
✅ **Feedback:** Clear pattern on what resonates + objections to solve  

If you get less:
- Was the email too long? Try shorter.
- Did they open it? Check open rates (Gmail, Mailchimp, etc.).
- Did they watch the video? Ask a follow-up question to find out.
- Is the product positioning clear? Adjust based on confusion.

---

## Critical Don'ts

❌ Don't send all 10 at once — batch and iterate  
❌ Don't say "beta" unless you mean it (they'll expect support)  
❌ Don't oversell — let the demo speak  
❌ Don't give up after one follow-up — homecare owners are busy  
❌ Don't pitch pricing yet — focus on solving the problem first
