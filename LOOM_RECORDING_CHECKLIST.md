# Loom Recording Checklist

## Before You Record

### Setup (15 minutes)
- [ ] Close all tabs except landing page + demo mockup
- [ ] Silence phone and Slack notifications
- [ ] Find quiet space with good lighting
- [ ] Test microphone (Loom will prompt you)
- [ ] Set screen to 1920x1080 minimum
- [ ] Open landing page at http://localhost:3000 (or live URL once deployed)
- [ ] Have DEMO_SCRIPT.md visible on second monitor or printed

### Preparation (10 minutes)
- [ ] Read through the script once, out loud
- [ ] Adjust tone: warm, confident, NOT robotic
- [ ] Identify 3 key pauses (problem reveal, example reply, payoff)
- [ ] Time yourself: should be 2:30-3:00 minutes

---

## Recording Flow (Step-by-step)

### SCENE 1: Problem (0:00 - 0:30)
**Screen:** Show landing page hero section  
**Action:**
1. Start Loom recording
2. Pause 2 seconds (let Loom start)
3. Read Scene 1 naturally (mention 5:47 PM call)
4. Keep landing page headline visible in background
5. Pause 1 second at end

**Script reminder:** "It's 5:47 PM on a Tuesday. Your phone buzzes..."

---

### SCENE 2: Status Quo (0:30 - 0:50)
**Screen:** Stay on landing page or show "Problem" section  
**Action:**
1. Transition smoothly
2. Read Scene 2 (manual process, 10 minutes per lead)
3. Pause 1 second at end

---

### SCENE 3: Product Demo (0:50 - 1:50)
**Screen:** Scroll to the mobile mockup on landing page  
**Action:**
1. Point to mobile mockup on screen
2. Read Scene 3: "Here's how it works: Step 1... Step 2... Step 3... Step 4..."
3. **PAUSE 3 SECONDS** on the mockup after mentioning it (let viewers read)
4. Pause 1 second at end

**Critical:** Make sure the mockup is centered and clearly visible

---

### SCENE 4: Real Example (1:50 - 2:30)
**Screen:** Keep mobile mockup visible  
**Action:**
1. Read Scene 4: "Here's a real example..."
2. Read the suggested reply FROM THE MOCKUP: "Hi there! Thanks for calling. We specialize in fall-risk care..."
3. **PAUSE 3 SECONDS** after reading (let it sink in)
4. Finish: "That's specific. It shows you know what they need..."
5. Pause 1 second at end

**Critical:** This is the EMOTIONAL moment. Don't rush.

---

### SCENE 5: Payoff (2:30 - 2:50)
**Screen:** Scroll to success message or form  
**Action:**
1. Read Scene 5: "One reply. 30 seconds..."
2. Keep it brief, let the impact land
3. Pause 1 second at end

---

### SCENE 6: CTA (2:50 - 3:00)
**Screen:** Scroll to "Join Waitlist" button  
**Action:**
1. Read Scene 6: "If you're tired of leads going cold..."
2. Point to or highlight the "Join Waitlist" button
3. Read: "Sign up to see the full demo"
4. Pause 2 seconds (let them absorb the CTA)
5. Stop recording

---

## Retakes: When to Re-record

**MUST re-record if:**
- [ ] You stumble over a word (pause and restart that scene)
- [ ] You rush the example (Scene 4) — it's the most important
- [ ] You lose the conversational tone (sounds robotic)
- [ ] Any technical glitch (screen lag, mockup not visible)

**OKAY if:**
- [ ] One "um" or "uh" — feels human
- [ ] Slight pauses between scenes — feels natural
- [ ] You vary the pacing slightly — people talk like that

- [ ] Verify mockup is visible for full Scene 3 & 4

## Common Mistakes (Avoid These)

❌ **Rushing Scene 4** — This is where homecare owners feel the impact  
❌ **Reading verbatim** — Use the script as a guide, not word-for-word  
❌ **No pauses** — Pauses let viewers absorb; they feel natural  
❌ **Showing your face** — Keep focus on screen/product (Loom studio mode optional)  
❌ **Saying "AI"** — Say "suggested replies" instead  
❌ **Over-explaining** — If homecare owners get it, stop there
