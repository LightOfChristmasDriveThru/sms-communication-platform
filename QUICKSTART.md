# 🚀 Quick Start: From Landing Page to 10 Homecare Owners

## Your Goal
Record a 3-minute demo and send it to 10 homecare owners this week.

---

## 📋 Pre-flight Checklist

Before you start, confirm you have:

- [ ] Landing page is live (or deployed to Vercel)
- [ ] DEMO_SCRIPT.md open in one window
- [ ] LOOM_RECORDING_CHECKLIST.md open in another
- [ ] Quiet space to record (no background noise)
- [ ] Loom account (free at loom.com)
- [ ] Gmail or email client ready for outreach
- [ ] OUTREACH_TRACKER.csv imported to Google Sheets

---

## 🎬 Step 1: Record the Demo (Today - 2 hours)

### 1a. Prepare (30 minutes)
1. Read DEMO_SCRIPT.md out loud once
2. Set up your screen (close extra tabs, Slack off, phone silent)
3. Open landing page at your live URL
4. Test Loom microphone
5. Plan your 3 key pauses:
   - After explaining the problem (0:30)
   - After showing the mockup (1:50)
   - After reading the example reply (2:30)

### 1b. Record (45 minutes)
1. Open Loom, hit record
2. Follow LOOM_RECORDING_CHECKLIST.md scene-by-scene
3. Don't aim for perfection — aim for natural
4. If you mess up a scene, pause and restart just that part
5. Target length: 2:50-3:00 minutes

### 1c. Post-Process (15 minutes)
1. Watch it once (don't obsess)
2. Confirm:
   - [ ] Audio is clear
   - [ ] Mobile mockup is visible
   - [ ] Example reply is readable
   - [ ] Tone sounds warm (not robotic)
3. Set to "Unlisted" (only people with link can watch)
4. Copy the Loom link

**Output:** Loom video link (looks like `https://www.loom.com/share/xxx`)

## 📊 Step 3: Build Your Target List (2 hours)

### 3a. Identify 10 Homecare Owners (1.5 hours)
Follow OUTREACH_PREP.md Step 1-2:

1. **Find them:**
   - Warm intros (ask your network)
   - LinkedIn search: "homecare owner" + your city
   - Google: "homecare agency [your city]"
   - Facebook groups: Join homecare groups

2. **Criteria:**
   - 1-20 person team (owner still involved)
   - In business 2+ years
   - Shows growth signals (hiring, expanding)
   - Has phone/email listed

3. **Organize by batch:**
   - Batch 1 (5): Warm intros (best response rate)
   - Batch 2 (3-4): Strong prospects (good response rate)
   - Batch 3 (1-2): Cold prospects (low but worth trying)

### 3b. Create Your Tracker (30 minutes)
1. Open OUTREACH_TRACKER.csv
2. Import to Google Sheets (easiest to share with yourself)
3. Fill in rows 1-10:
   - Company Name
   - Owner/Manager Name
   - Email
   - Phone
   - City
   - Source (how you found them)
   - Batch (1, 2, or 3)
4. Save and share the link with yourself

**Output:** Google Sheets link with your 10 targets

## 📧 Step 4: Send Emails (3 days, spread across a week)

### 4a. Batch 1: Warm Intros (Day 1)
1. Open OUTREACH_EMAIL_TEMPLATE.md
2. Customize Email 1 for each person:
   - Replace [INSERT LOOM LINK] with your video link
   - Replace [Your Name] with your actual name
   - Add a personal touch (mention mutual contact if applicable)
3. Send to 5 people
4. Update OUTREACH_TRACKER.csv: Date Sent = today
5. **BONUS:** Call them 2 hours after emailing
   - Say: "Did you get my email about the 3-minute demo?"
   - If yes: "Awesome, would love to hear what you think"
   - Sets expectation they'll watch

### 4b. Batch 2: Strong Prospects (Day 3-4)
1. Repeat process for 3-4 companies with growth signals
2. Mention something specific in the email:
   - "I saw you're hiring" or "Your team is growing fast"
   - Shows you did research (much better response rate)
3. Update tracker

### 4c. Batch 3: Remaining (Day 7-8)
1. Send to remaining 1-2 cold prospects
2. Keep it short and one-sentence hook
3. Plan to follow up in 3 days if no response

**Output:** Emails sent, tracker updated with "Date Sent"

---

## 📲 Step 5: Track Responses (Daily for 2 weeks)

### Each Morning
1. Check your email for responses
2. Update OUTREACH_TRACKER.csv:
   - Email Opened? (Gmail shows this)
   - Responded? (Did they reply?)
   - Video Watched? (Did they say they watched it?)
   - Response Date
   - Sentiment (Excited / Interested / Not sure / Not relevant)
   - Key Objection (If they raised concerns)

### If They Didn't Respond (After 5 days)
1. Send Email 2 (follow-up) from OUTREACH_EMAIL_TEMPLATE.md
2. Keep it warm, not pushy
3. Update tracker: Follow-up Sent Date

### If They Responded Positively
1. Reply to their objection (use OUTREACH_EMAIL_TEMPLATE.md objection handling)
2. Schedule a 15-minute call
3. Update tracker: Call Scheduled = Yes, Call Date = [date]

---

## 📞 Step 6: Schedule & Run Calls (Week 2-3)

### Before Each Call
1. Review their company (website, team size, recent posts)
2. Review their objection from the email
3. Pull up the 5-minute call script from OUTREACH_PREP.md

### During Call (15 minutes)
1. Thank them for watching
2. Ask: "When a lead comes in after hours, what's your go-to move?"
3. Listen — this is THE insight
4. Explain how your product solves their specific problem
5. Ask: "Does that solve it for you?"
6. If yes: "Would you try it for 2 weeks if I set it up?"

### After Call
1. Update tracker:
   - Call Notes (what they said)
   - Willing to Beta (yes/no/maybe)
   - Next Step
   - Follow-up Date

## 📊 Step 7: Measure & Learn (End of Week 3)

Calculate these metrics from your tracker:

```
Video Watch Rate = (# watched video) / 10
Response Rate = (# replied to email) / 10
Call Rate = (# scheduled calls) / (# replied)
Beta Rate = (# willing to beta) / 10
```

**Example results:**
- 7 out of 10 watched video = 70% (good!)
- 3 out of 7 replied = 43% (good!)
- 2 out of 3 scheduled calls = 67% (good!)
- 1 out of 10 willing to beta = 10% (target: 1-3)

### Learnings to Extract
1. **What resonated?** (Which companies watched?)
2. **What confused?** (What were the objections?)
3. **What's the hook?** (One sentence that made them watch?)
4. **Who's your customer?** (Batch 1 / Batch 2 / Batch 3?)

## 🎯 Success Criteria

✅ **You've Won If:**
- 5+ people watched the video
- 2+ people scheduled calls
- 1+ person willing to beta test

🟡 **You're on Track If:**
- 3-4 people watched
- 1+ person showed interest
- Consistent feedback on what works/doesn't work

❌ **Something Needs to Change If:**
- <3 people watched
- No one replied
- Tons of "not interested" responses

**If results are low:** Adjust your email (make it shorter, more personal). Don't give up.

## 🚨 Common Mistakes (Avoid These)

❌ **Sending all 10 at once** — You can't iterate. Send in batches.  
❌ **Generic emails** — "Hi, check out this demo" gets ignored.  
❌ **No follow-up** — If they don't respond in 3 days, follow up.  
❌ **Waiting for perfection** — Record and send, iterate on feedback.  
❌ **No phone calls** — Email gets 20% response. Phone gets 60%.  
❌ **Forgetting to update tracker** — Do it same day while it's fresh.
