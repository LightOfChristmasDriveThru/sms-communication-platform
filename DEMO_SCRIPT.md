# Demo Script: Missed Lead Responder
## For Loom Recording (2-3 minutes)

---

## SCENE 1: The Problem (30 seconds)
**[Camera/Screen: Show your phone, 5:47 PM]**

> "It's 5:47 PM on a Tuesday. Your phone buzzes. A family is calling about in-home care for their mother—she's 78, had a fall last month, and they're looking for help TODAY.
>
> But you're wrapping up visits. Your team is in the field. By the time you call them back at 8 AM tomorrow, they've already booked with someone else.
>
> This happens 2, 3, maybe 5 times a week. Each one is a lost consult. Each one is revenue your team needed."

**[Visual: Show a missed call notification or text message]**

---

## SCENE 2: The Status Quo (20 seconds)
**[Screen: Inbox overflowing, or missed lead notes scattered]**

> "Right now, you catch these leads manually. You read the message, craft a reply from scratch, and hope you send it fast enough. It takes 10 minutes per lead.
>
> Or they go cold. And nobody remembers to follow up."

---

## SCENE 3: Enter the Product (1 minute)
**[Screen: Open landing page, then show the demo flow]**

> "This is Respond Fast. Here's how it works:
>
> **Step 1:** Your lead comes in—phone call, email, web form, wherever.
> 
> **Step 2:** The system detects you didn't answer in 2 minutes. It shows you a suggested reply—already written, already tailored to this lead and your team's voice.
>
> **Step 3:** You read it in 30 seconds. If it's perfect, one tap and it's sent. If you want to tweak it, you edit and send.
>
> **Step 4:** The lead hears back in 30 seconds instead of 8 hours. They book the consult with you, not your competitor."

**[Show the mobile mockup on screen: lead message → suggested reply → send button]**

---

## SCENE 4: Real Example (40 seconds)
**[Screen: Show the fall-risk scenario side-by-side]**

> "Here's a real example. A daughter calls about her 78-year-old mom—fall risk, needs daytime care. It's after hours.
>
> Instead of 'Let me look into this and get back to you,' she sees THIS:
>
> *[Read the suggested reply]*
>
> 'Hi there! Thanks for calling. We specialize in fall-risk care and support for seniors living independently. Let's schedule a quick intake call tomorrow morning. Does 10 AM work for you?'
>
> That's specific. It shows you know what they need. And the family books the call on the spot instead of texting 3 other agencies."

---

## SCENE 5: The Payoff (20 seconds)
**[Screen: Show the waitlist form or success screen]**

> "One reply. 30 seconds. And suddenly you're not losing leads to slower teams.
>
> Over a month, that's 10, 15, 20 extra consults. For a homecare owner, that's real money. And real peace of mind."

---

## SCENE 6: Call to Action (10 seconds)
**[Screen: Show landing page CTA or form]**

> "If you're tired of leads going cold, let's talk. We're launching this month. Sign up to see the full demo."

**[Show "Join Waitlist" button]**

## RECORDING TIPS

**Pacing:**
- Speak clearly, slightly slower than normal (demo videos need breathing room)
- Pause 1-2 seconds after each section so viewers can absorb
- Use your actual voice (not robotic); this is for homecare owners who know the pain

**Screen Recording:**
- 1080p minimum
- Show mobile mockup prominently (that's the whole product)
- Pause on the suggested reply for 3 seconds so viewers read it
- Use laptop/desktop to show the page, then phone mockup to show the interaction

**Music/Audio:**
- Background music optional (keeps energy up) — something light, professional
- No background noise; find a quiet space
- Mic close enough that your voice is clear

**Length Target:**
- 2:30 to 3:00 minutes total
- 30 sec = problem
- 20 sec = status quo
- 60 sec = product demo
- 40 sec = real example
- 20 sec = payoff
- 10 sec = CTA

---

## Strongest Objection to Handle
**"How does it know how to write like our team?"**

> "Great question. The system learns from 2–3 of your replies that you write once. From there, it suggests replies that match your voice—specific to homecare, warm but professional, action-oriented. You always review before sending. It's your brand, your words."

*(Include this in a follow-up email or slide if needed.)*

---

## What to Avoid
- ❌ Don't oversell ("AI" — just say "suggested replies")
- ❌ Don't make it too technical — homecare owners care about one thing: booked consults
- ❌ Don't rush the example — let it land
- ❌ Don't assume they know what "missed leads" are — they DO, that's why they're watching

---

## Success Metric
After watching, the viewer should think:

> "That's exactly my problem. And 30 seconds is faster than I could do it. I should try this."
