That gap? That's lost revenue.

You always review before sending. It's your brand, your words. We're not replacing you; we're speeding you up.

## Key Principles for Each Email

1. **Keep it short** — Homecare owners are busy. 3-4 sentences max.
2. **Lead with pain** — Start with their problem, not your solution
3. **One ask** — Watch the video OR reply OR schedule a call. Not all three.
4. **Be human** — Use "I" and "you," not "we" and "customers"
5. **Include your number** — Text often works better than email for busy owners
6. **Follow-up is critical** — 2-3 emails is normal, not annoying
