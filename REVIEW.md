# Quality Review Checklist

Use this checklist before showing anything to homecare owners.

## Landing Page

- [ ] **5-second test**: A homecare owner knows what this does in 5 seconds (not "platform", not "automation")
- [ ] **Problem is real**: We name a specific pain (missed leads, lost revenue, team stress)
- [ ] **Solution is clear**: One sentence explaining how we solve it
- [ ] **CTA is obvious**: Button stands out, next step is clear
- [ ] **Mobile looks good**: Heading, image, CTA all readable on phone
- [ ] **No jargon**: No "AI", "async", "NLP", "integrate", "workflow"
- [ ] **Homecare language**: Uses words owners use ("lead", "response time", "consult", "busy", "after-hours")

## Demo Flow

- [ ] **Mobile works**: Demo plays and is navigable on phone
- [ ] **Scenario is real**: A homecare owner recognizes this as their problem
- [ ] **Each step is clear**: No ambiguity about what's happening
- [ ] **Pace is right**: Not too fast, not too slow (2-3 min total?)
- [ ] **Audio is clear**: No background noise, easy to hear
- [ ] **Specificity wins**: Show a real lead message, not placeholder text
- [ ] **Outcome is obvious**: "Consult booked" or "lead didn't go cold"

## Copy Review

- [ ] **No marketing fluff**: No "revolutionize", "game-changer", "seamless"
- [ ] **Owner perspective**: Written from owner's point of view, not company's
- [ ] **Problem-first**: Starts with the pain, not the product
- [ ] **Benefit over feature**: "Never lose a lead" vs. "AI-powered response templates"

## Before Sending to Customers

- [ ] **Tested on actual phone**: Demo plays smoothly on iPhone + Android
- [ ] **Tested on wifi + 4G**: No buffering issues
- [ ] **Loom link works**: Click and play, no auth needed
- [ ] **Thank you message**: Set expectations ("We'll review this week", etc.)
- [ ] **Warm intro**: Email has context, not generic
- [ ] **One ask**: "Can we schedule 15 min to get your feedback?" Not "Can you also share with 5 friends"

---

## If something doesn't pass

If you can't check a box above, don't send it yet. Fix it and re-check.  
Ship > Perfect. But broken demo or unclear copy isn't ship-ready.
