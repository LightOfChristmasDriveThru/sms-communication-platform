# Demo Flow Spec

## Goal

Show a homecare owner how they'll use this product in 2-3 minutes.  
Make it specific and real. Use actual lead messages, not placeholder text.

## Scenario

**Situation**: It's 5:15pm on a Tuesday. Your team is finishing their visits. A new lead comes in via phone/email/web form. Nobody's at the desk to answer.

## Flow

### Step 1: Lead Arrives (0:00-0:15)
- **Show**: Lead notification (phone screen or email)
- **Example lead message**:
  - Phone transcription: "Hi, my mom needs care starting next Monday. She has a fall risk history..."
  - OR email: "Need in-home care for my mother. She's 82, cognitive decline, needs 24-hr monitoring..."
  - OR web form submission: Name, phone, age, condition summary
- **Narration**: "It's 5:15pm. Your lead comes in. Your team's finishing their visits. Nobody picks up."

### Step 2: System Notices (0:15-0:25)
- **Show**: Quick dashboard alert or notification
- **What it says**: "New lead: [name]. No response yet. Suggest reply?"
- **Narration**: "The system notices this lead hasn't been answered."

### Step 3: Suggested Reply (0:25-0:50)
- **Show**: The system's suggested reply to the lead
- **Example reply**:
  - "Hi [name], thanks for reaching out about care for your mom. We specialize in fall risk and cognitive support. Let's get her started. Can we schedule an intake call for tomorrow at 10am?"
- **Show**: Owner reading it (takes 5 seconds to review)
- **Narration**: "We suggest a reply in 30 seconds based on the lead and your homecare's style."

### Step 4: Owner Sends (0:50-1:10)
- **Show**: Owner clicks "Send" or edits slightly and sends
- **Action**: Reply goes out to the lead (SMS, email, or call-back message)
- **Narration**: "You send it in 30 seconds. No need to write from scratch."

### Step 5: Follow-Up & Booked Consult (1:10-1:30)
- **Show**: Timeline view
  - 5:15pm - Lead came in
  - 5:16pm - Your reply sent
  - 5:30pm - Lead responds: "Tomorrow at 10am works great!"
  - 5:31pm - You confirm the appointment
- **Result**: "Consult booked. No leads lost to slow response."
- **Narration**: "You recover a lead that would've gone cold. Consult booked."

### Step 6: Call to Action (1:30-end)
- **Show**: Demo ends
- **Message**: "Join the waitlist to try this with your own leads"
- **Button/Link**: To landing page or waitlist form

## Key Details

### Authenticity
- Use real lead messages from homecare situations (not generic examples)
- Use realistic reply style (warm, specific, action-oriented)
- Show actual time markers (5:15pm, 5:16pm, etc.) to make it feel real

### Pacing
- Each step is clear and separate
- Total runtime: 2-3 minutes (not rushed, not slow)
- Pause on key moments (the suggested reply, the "consult booked" result)

### Mobile First
- This demo must work on phone screens
- Text is readable at phone size
- No tiny UI elements
- Tap targets are big enough

### Audio
- Clear, friendly narration (not robotic)
- Background music optional but not distracting
- No "bing" sounds or beeps

## Recording Notes

- Use screen recording tool (Loom, Screen Studio, or similar)
- Narrate live (not overdubbed text-to-speech if you can help it)
- If showing a mobile app: use actual device or high-quality simulator
- Test on phone before uploading
- Make sure Loom link doesn't require sign-up to view

## What NOT to show

- Admin panels or settings
- Multiple workflows or branching logic
- How the reply gets generated (that's backend magic)
- Pricing or billing
- Security features or compliance
- Integration complexity

## Success Criteria

- [ ] Owner recognizes this as their problem
- [ ] Owner sees themselves using it
- [ ] Owner wants to try it
- [ ] Owner sends this to a colleague ("you need to see this")
