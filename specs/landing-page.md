# Landing Page Spec

## Goal

Get homecare owners to join the waitlist. They should understand what this solves in 5 seconds.

## Structure

### Hero Section
- **Headline**: (Your version here - should start with the problem, not the product)
- **Subheading**: One sentence on how this saves them revenue
- **Visual**: Screenshot or illustration showing the problem (e.g., "5:47pm — Lead comes in, nobody picks up")
- **CTA**: "Join waitlist" button (high contrast)

### Problem Section
- **Headline**: "How many leads go cold?"
- **Pain points** (3 specific):
  1. Lead comes in after 5pm or during busy hours
  2. Team forgets to call back, or calls back too late
  3. Prospect books with competitor instead
- **Owner quote** (if you have one): Real homecare owner talking about this problem

### Solution Section
- **Headline**: "Respond in minutes, not hours"
- **How it works** (3 steps, simple):
  1. Lead comes in → we alert you
  2. We suggest a reply in 30 seconds
  3. You send it → consult is booked
- **Visual**: Screen recording or animation of the flow

### Waitlist CTA
- **Headline**: "Be first to recover your missed leads"
- **Button**: "Join waitlist"
- **Optional**: "Email us with questions" link

## Tone & Language

- Use "you" (homecare owner), not "organizations"
- Reference real problems: "calls go to voicemail", "team is swamped at 4-5pm", "lost a consult last week"
- No tech jargon: AI, automation, integration, workflow, async
- No marketing-speak: game-changer, revolutionize, seamless, next-gen

## Copy Samples (starter)

| Section | Avoid | Use Instead |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| Headline | "Intelligent Lead Response Platform" | "Never miss another lead" |
| Problem | "Asynchronous communication gaps" | "Your team's too busy. Leads book elsewhere." |
| Solution | "Leverage AI to automate responses" | "Suggest a reply in 30 seconds. You send it." |

## Tech Notes

- Responsive design: mobile-first
- Fast load: <2s on 4G
- No external dependencies (keep it simple)
- Works without JavaScript (if possible)
